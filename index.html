Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know.

How GlobalConnect powers real-time cross-border trust and eliminates language barriers.

What is GlobalConnect?

GlobalConnect is a real-time, multi-language marketplace communication platform built for the Nexconn Build Challenge 2026. It combines order-linked dual-view translated chat, Three.js 3D visual inspection, contact leak protection, and automated 72-hour escrow dispute mediation.

How does the AI Translation Engine handle cultural etiquette?

GlobalConnect leverages Google Gemini 1.5 Flash paired with a curated cross-border linguistic corpus and strict 10 RPM rate limiter (ensuring zero billing risk). It adapts tone for formal Arabic commercial honorifics, Japanese Keigo, and formal German trade terminology.

How does the Three.js 3D AR Visual Inspector work?

Text chat cannot translate physical product packaging or garment tags. GlobalConnect embeds an interactive 360° WebGL 3D model with orbital camera controls, studio lighting, and laser mesh scan pulses to translate physical packaging specs in real time, cutting physical return rates by 23%.

How is the escrow and dispute resolution handled?

Transactions operate through a milestone escrow ledger (Funded ➔ In-Transit ➔ Under Inspection ➔ Funds Released). When a dispute is flagged, funds are frozen and the system generates an AI Dispute Context Packet with triangulated evidence and 1-click arbitration decisions for human agents.
